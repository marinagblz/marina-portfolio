A data cleaning and exploratory analysis project based on a fictional hotel reservation dataset, with a focus on Revenue Management and hotel performance indicators.

Overview

From messy data to revenue insights

The project simulates the workflow of a Revenue Management Analyst: starting with a deliberately messy dataset, cleaning and validating the data, analysing booking and demand patterns, and presenting the results through Python and Power BI.

The objective of this project is to explore how hotel performance and booking behaviour change throughout the year and across different demand segments. The analysis focuses on: seasonality and hotel performance, Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR, booking lead time, length of stay, market segments, distribution channels, events and their relationship with ADR, cancellation patterns
